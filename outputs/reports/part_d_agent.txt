## Task 5 - tools
  get_order(order_id) - Look up one order in the Instamart database by its order id.
  search_policy(query) - Retrieve the three policy chunks most relevant to a query.
  calculate_refund(order_amount, delivery_minutes) - Compute the refund: 100% of the amount if delivery took more than 45 minutes, else 0.
  issue_refund(order_id, refund_inr) - Issue a refund of at most INR 1000 and record it in the refund ledger.
  escalate_to_human(order_id, refund_inr) - Send a refund above INR 1000 to a human support lead and record it in the ledger.
TOOL_MAP: get_order, search_policy, calculate_refund, issue_refund, escalate_to_human
